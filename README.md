# Pop-Up Workspace Aggregator - Business Analysis

## Project Overview
**Pop-Up Workspace** is an aggregator business model designed to provide on-demand workspaces for modern professionals, budding startups, and remote workers. The platform offers flexible workstyles through various subscription and rental tiers.

---

## 📊 Financial Projections
The business demonstrates steady growth in revenue and net profit margins over its first two years of operation.

## 📈 Unit Economics & Growth

### Break-Even Analysis
* **Target:** The business requires **197 customers per month** to cover all operational costs and reach the break-even point.

### Customer Acquisition & Lifetime Value
The marketing spend is set at **₹10,833 per month**, acquiring approximately 15 new customers monthly, resulting in a **CAC of ₹722.2**.

## 💡 Practical Insights
1.  **High-Margin Segments:** Event Space Rentals and Premium Subscriptions offer the healthiest LTV/CAC ratios, indicating strong scalability in these areas.
2.  **Margin Growth:** While initial net margins are thin (5-7%), the business model shows potential for long-term profitability as the customer base scales beyond the 197-user break-even threshold.
